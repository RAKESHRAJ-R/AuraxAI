1 · What This System Is

Aura is an AI sales assistant that replies to customers on WhatsApp for Theaurax.in. It answers questions, searches the live product catalogue, takes orders, sends payment links, and raises support tickets — around the clock, in English and in Tanglish.

1.1 The three parts

PartWhat it doesWho touches it
The bot Runs on the server, stays logged in to your WhatsApp number, and replies to every customer message automatically. Nobody — it runs unattended.
The admin console The web page you sign in to. Four sections: Monitor, WhatsApp, Knowledge Hub, Support Tickets. You and your team, daily.
Your WooCommerce store The real source of products, prices, stock and orders. The bot reads from it and creates real orders in it. Managed as usual in WordPress.

1.2 What the assistant can do

  • Greet customers and answer common questions instantly (COD, delivery time, sizing, returns, customization, bulk orders, tracking, jersey care, kids sizes).
  • Search the live product catalogue and show matching jerseys with price and stock.
  • Take a size and quantity, collect a shipping address, and confirm an order.
  • Create a real WooCommerce order and send the customer a direct payment link.
  • Look up an existing order's status for the customer who placed it.
  • Raise a support ticket for wrong items, damage, delays, exchanges, or a request to speak to a human — and alert you on WhatsApp the moment it happens.
  • Alert you for bulk enquiries above your configured quantity threshold.
  • Re-engage a customer who went quiet mid-conversation.
  • Reply in Tanglish to a customer who writes in Tanglish, for the whole conversation.

1.3 What it deliberately will not do

  • It will not promise a refund, a discount, or a specific resolution to a complaint — it logs a ticket and tells the customer the team will follow up.
  • It will not invent a tracking number that does not exist yet.
  • It will not share an order's details with a WhatsApp number that did not place it.
  • It will not answer from an uploaded document if the document does not actually cover the question — it falls back to answering normally.

1.4 How a single customer message gets answered

Understanding this order matters, because it explains why your corrections take effect the way they do. The bot tries the cheapest, most reliable route first and only falls through to the AI model when it has to.

Customer message arrives on WhatsApp │ ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 1. Your Knowledge Hub answer (Teach tab) │ ← highest priority │ Strong keyword match → replied instantly, no AI │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │ no strong match ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 2. Built-in FAQ (14 standard topics, EN + Tanglish) │ │ Only when the chat is idle → replied instantly │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │ not an FAQ ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 3. Shortcuts: "M size 2" · "2 M 5" · "yes" │ │ Read directly as cart / confirmation, no AI │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │ not a shortcut ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 4. AI assistant │ │ + your soft-matching Knowledge Hub answers │ │ + top 3 relevant chunks from documents / website │ │ + tools: search products, cart, address, order, │ │ order lookup, support ticket, escalate │ └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │ ▼ Reply sent back to the customer
The single most important consequence A Knowledge Hub answer beats everything else, including the built-in FAQ. If the bot is saying something wrong, teaching it the right answer in the Teach tab overrides the old behaviour immediately — no restart, no developer.

3 · Monitor — Watching the Bot Work

The Monitor page is your instrument panel. Nothing here is a setting — it is all read-only. Statistics refresh every 5 seconds and the server log every 2.5 seconds.

3.1 The four tiles at the top

🖧 API Requests

Total AI requests made since the bot last started, split into success and errors underneath.

Read it as: a few errors alongside many successes is normal — the bot retries and switches providers automatically. Errors climbing while successes stay flat means every provider is failing; check the server log and the key list.

🧮 Tokens Used

Total text volume processed across all AI providers since restart. Tokens are how AI usage is billed and rate-limited — roughly one token per ¾ of a word.

Read it as: your cost and quota meter. A sudden jump without a matching jump in chats usually means long conversations, not more customers.

💬 Active Chats

How many customer conversations are currently held in memory — people the bot is mid-conversation with.

🧠 Active Provider

Which AI service answered most recently, and which of its keys. You may see Groq, Sarvam, Fireworks, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Gemini here.

Read it as: switching between providers is designed behaviour, not a fault. The bot moves down a fallback chain whenever one provider is busy or out of quota. "None" simply means no AI call has been made since the last restart.

3.2 Call Traffic & Token Usage

The green-and-red line chart covers the last 24 hours in eight 3-hour blocks. Green is successful calls, red is failed ones. Under it, one bar per provider shows how much of its assumed allowance has been consumed.

What the provider bars really mean Those percentages are measured against a built-in reference allowance per provider, not a live reading from the provider's own billing. Use them to compare providers against each other and to spot an unusual spike — not as an exact bill.

3.3 API Keys & Cooldowns

One row per key the bot holds, named provider · Key #0, #1 and so on.

IndicatorMeaning
ActiveThe key is available for use right now.
Cooldown 42m 10s That key hit its daily or per-minute limit. The bot has parked it and is using the others. It comes back automatically when the timer runs out — no action needed.
Green dot beside the nameThis is the key that served the most recent call.

When to worry: only when every key of every provider shows a cooldown at the same time. Then customers are getting the "we're getting a lot of messages" reply, and their questions are being queued for automatic retry.

3.5 Server console

The dark panel at the bottom is the live server log — the last 250 lines, auto-scrolling. Grey is informational, amber is a warning, red is an error. Scroll up and it stops following so you can read; scroll back to the bottom and it resumes.

Lines worth recognising:

Line starts withMeaning

[Retrieval Service] …Something about your uploaded documents or crawled website.

Anything red and repeatingCopy the line and send it to your developer. That is the useful thing to report.

4.2 Pairing a number for the first time

1
Wait for the page to show Scan QR code and display the QR image.
2
On the phone that owns the business number, open WhatsApp.
3
Tap Menu (Android) or Settings (iPhone) → Linked devices → Link a device.
4
Point the phone at the QR code on screen.
5

The QR code expires If it has been on screen a while and scanning does nothing, wait — the page refreshes every 2.5 seconds and a fresh code appears on its own. Scan the new one.
Unread chats already on the phone WILL be answered If you connect a number with, say, 1,000 unread conversations sitting on it, the bot finds every one where the customer is still waiting for a reply and answers them — recent ones straight away, older ones gradually over the following hours.

You will get a WhatsApp summary within a minute of connecting telling you how many were found. Nothing is discarded. See section 8.7 for exactly how it is paced.

4.3 The linked-account panel

Once connected, the page shows exactly which account is paired. This exists so you never have to check "Linked devices" on every handset in the shop to find out which phone is running the bot.

FieldWhat it shows
Linked numberThe WhatsApp number customers are messaging, e.g. +91 98765 43210.
Account nameThe WhatsApp profile name on that account.
DeviceThe phone's platform (Android, iPhone).
Linked sinceHow long the current session has been connected.

This panel appears only while connected. It is deliberately hidden when the badge is red, so a stale number can never sit next to a "Disconnected" badge and mislead you.

4.4 🔌 Log out this number

Unlinks the phone from the bot. Use it when moving the bot to a different number, or if you suspect the session has been compromised.

This stops the bot answering customers From the moment you confirm until someone scans a new QR code, every incoming customer message goes unanswered. The confirmation dialog names the exact number so you cannot unlink the wrong one by accident. Do it at a quiet hour, and have the new phone in hand.

What happens when you click it:

  • A confirmation appears naming the number. Cancel is safe.
  • The session is revoked on the phone and cleared on the server — the bot cannot silently re-pair the same number afterwards.
  • After about two seconds the bot restarts its connection and a fresh QR code appears on the same page. No server restart is needed.
  • If the unlink fails, the page says so plainly and tells you what is needed — it will not claim a clean unlink that did not happen.

4.5 Safe mode

The grey banner at the bottom of the page is a reminder that safe mode may be on. When the server is configured with a list of tester numbers, the bot replies only to those numbers and silently ignores everyone else.

The classic false alarm "The bot is connected but customers get no reply" is almost always safe mode still switched on after testing. It is a server setting, not a button on this page — ask your developer to clear the tester-number list when you go live.

5 · Knowledge Hub — Teaching the Bot

This is the section that makes the assistant yours. Everything you save here goes live immediately — no restart, no deploy, no developer. It has three tabs.

TabUse it to…
📚 Teach the botWrite exact answers to exact questions. The most direct and most reliable control you have.
🗂 Knowledge sourcesUpload documents or crawl your website so the bot can read from them.
🔎 Review mistakesRead real conversations that went wrong and fix them on the spot.

5.1 The "Teach the bot" tab

The form at the top adds or edits one answer. Below it is every answer you have saved.

Customer question

One realistic example of how a customer would ask. This is a label for you — it helps you find the entry later, and it is what appears in the saved list. It is not what the matching is based on.

Example: Do you deliver to Sri Lanka?

Correct answer

Word for word what the bot should reply. When the match is strong, this text is sent to the customer exactly as written. Write it in the tone you want customers to hear — complete sentences, your own phrasing, emoji if you like.

Keep it to a WhatsApp-sized reply. Two or three sentences beats a paragraph.

Trigger keywords — comma separated

This is the field that actually decides when your answer is used. The question field does not; the keywords do. Getting these right is the whole skill.

Two kinds of keyword behave differently:

  • Phrases (two or more words, e.g. sri lanka) are a strong signal. One phrase match is enough for the bot to reply with your answer directly.
  • Single words (e.g. international) are a weaker signal. It takes two different single words matching for a direct reply. A single word on its own only nudges the AI toward your answer.

Practical rule: give every entry at least one phrase, plus three or four single words.

Language
SettingUse when
Both (default)Almost always. The answer is available to every customer.
English onlyThe answer is written in English and would read oddly to a Tanglish customer.
Tanglish onlyThe answer is written in Tanglish.

To answer the same question properly in both languages, create two entries — one English-only, one Tanglish-only, each with keywords in its own language. The bot picks the one matching the customer's locked language.

Active checkbox

Ticked, the bot uses this answer. Unticked, it is saved but ignored — useful for a seasonal answer (a festival offer, a stock pause) you want to switch back on later rather than retype.

💾 Save answer stores it and it is live on the very next customer message. Saving with an empty answer is refused.

5.2 The "🗂 Knowledge sources" tab

Where you give the bot material to read: your website, or files you upload. Part 6 covers which to use; this section covers how.

🌐 Website

FieldWhat to enter
URL The page to start from. Point it at the most specific page that holds the information — https://theaurax.in/shipping-policy, not just https://theaurax.in.
Max pages
default 15		 How many pages the crawl may read before stopping. Keeps a crawl from running away.
Link depth
default 2		 How many clicks away from the starting page it may travel. 0 = that one page only. 1 = it and the pages it links to. 2 = one step further.

🌐 Crawl & index starts it. The button reads "Crawling…" while it works — this can take a minute or two. When it finishes you get a message like "Indexed 6 page(s) — 41 chunks."

Depth 2 from the homepage is usually a waste A real crawl of theaurax.in at 15 pages / depth 2 spent its entire budget on product-category grids and empty account pages, and never reached the shipping, returns or about pages — the exact prose the feature exists to index. The crawler follows links in alphabetical order, so category pages consume the budget first.

Do this instead: crawl your policy pages one at a time at depth 0. Three precise crawls beat one broad one every time.
Re-crawling the same URL does not replace the old copy It creates a second source, and the bot then searches both — near-identical duplicates competing with each other. Always delete the existing source first, then crawl the URL again.

📄 Document

Choose a file and click 📄 Upload & index.

RequirementDetail
Accepted typesPDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML
Maximum size20 MB per file
Must contain real text A scanned or photographed PDF will be rejected. If you can select and copy the text in a PDF reader, it will work; if it is a picture of a page, it will not. Re-export it from Word, or retype it into a TXT file.

Uploaded files are read into memory, indexed, and never written to the server's disk.

5.3 The "🔎 Review mistakes" tab

A list of real conversations that show a sign of trouble. A chat is flagged when it contains:

  • an error or fallback reply — the bot said it couldn't process something;
  • a repeated question — the customer asked the same thing twice or more, which almost always means the first answer missed;
  • an abandoned cart — they got partway through buying and stopped.

Each card shows the customer, the reasons it was flagged, and the last few turns of the real conversation. Read them — this is the single best source of ideas for what to teach.

✏️ Teach the right answer jumps you to the Teach tab with the customer's actual question already filled in and keywords suggested from it. Write the answer, check the keywords, save. This is the core improvement loop of the whole system.

5.4 The "needs answer" queue and the red badge

Every 30 minutes the server scans conversations for questions the bot stumbled on, and drafts each one as an inactive entry at the top of your Teach tab. The red number on the sidebar is how many are waiting. You also get a WhatsApp ping when new ones appear.

Two ways to clear one:

ActionUse whenResult
✏️ AnswerIt is a real question worth answering. Write the reply and save. It becomes a live answer and the badge drops by one.
DismissIt is noise — a typo, a wrong number, something irrelevant. Permanently removed. It will never be suggested again, even though the old conversation still exists.
Use Dismiss freely Dismiss exists precisely so the queue stays meaningful. A queue full of junk you keep scrolling past is a queue you will stop reading. Ten minutes a week keeping it at zero is the highest-value maintenance you can do on this system.

6 · Choosing the Right Knowledge Option

Three ways to give the bot information, and picking the wrong one is the most common reason people conclude "it doesn't work". This part exists to make the choice obvious.

6.1 The three options at a glance

📚 Teach (Q&A)
Exact answers, word for word
  • Highest priority — beats everything
  • Replies instantly, no AI involved
  • You control the exact wording
  • One question at a time
  • Live the moment you save
📄 Document
Reference material to read from
  • Feeds the AI, doesn't override it
  • Handles many facts in one upload
  • Wording is the AI's, not yours
  • Needs selectable text
  • Best for tables and lists
🌐 Website
Your live pages, indexed
  • Same behaviour as a document
  • No file to prepare
  • Only as good as the page's text
  • Does not auto-refresh
  • Best for existing policy pages

6.2 The decision tree

START: what do you want the bot to know? │ ├─ Is there ONE right answer you want said a specific way? │ (a price, a policy, a phone number, a promise) │ │ │ └─► ✅ TEACH TAB (Q&A). Nothing else guarantees the wording. │ ├─ Is it many facts — a table, a chart, a long policy? │ │ │ ├─ Does a page on your site already contain it, │ │ written as readable text? │ │ │ │ │ ├─ YES ─► ✅ WEBSITE CRAWL of that exact page (depth 0) │ │ │ │ │ └─ NO ─► ✅ DOCUMENT UPLOAD │ │ │ └─ Is it in a spreadsheet / image / scan? │ └─► Convert to PDF or DOCX with real text, then UPLOAD │ ├─ Is it changing this week (an offer, a stock pause)? │ └─► ✅ TEACH TAB. Easy to edit, easy to switch off. │ └─ Did the bot get something WRONG in a real chat? └─► ✅ REVIEW TAB → "Teach the right answer"

6.4 Website crawl or document upload? The five-question test

QuestionYes →No →
Is the information already on a public page of your site?WebsiteDocument
Is that page mostly readable paragraphs, not a grid of product tiles?WebsiteDocument
Will the page's wording change often?Document*Website
Is it a table, chart, or list of measurements?DocumentEither

Edit the page on your website, and the bot keeps answering from the old text until someone deletes the source and crawls it again. Put a reminder in your calendar whenever you change a policy page.

7 · Support Tickets — After-Sales

When a customer has a problem the bot cannot solve, it raises a ticket and alerts you on WhatsApp straight away. This page is the shared list your team works from. It refreshes every 10 seconds.

7.1 How a ticket gets created

The bot raises one on its own when a customer reports an after-sales issue. Before doing so it apologises, collects their name, the order ID and a short description, and asks for a photo when the issue is a wrong, damaged or misprinted item. Then it:

  • creates the ticket with a reference like TKT-1043;
  • sends you the full details on WhatsApp immediately, photo included;
  • gives the customer the reference number and the support email;
  • promises only that the team will follow up — never a refund or a specific outcome.

7.4 Working the list

  • Open / Resolved / All switches the view. It opens on Open — your work queue.
  • The search box filters by name, phone, ticket ID, order number, issue type or any word in the description. Searching the order number is the fast way to find a returning customer's history.
  • Resolve marks it done and removes it from the Open list and the sidebar badge. Mark it resolved only after you have actually contacted the customer — the bot does not tell them anything when you click it.
  • Reopen puts a resolved ticket back in the queue if the customer comes back about it.
A working rhythm Clear Wrong item, Damaged and Missing package first — they cost you a customer if they sit. Then Talk to human: that customer is already frustrated and the bot has told them a person is coming. Aim to end each day with the sidebar badge at zero.

8 · What the Bot Does on Its Own

Nothing in this part needs managing. It is here so that when a customer forwards you a screenshot, you know exactly why the bot said what it said.

8.1 Instant answers

Four kinds of message are handled in code, with no AI call at all — which is why they are fast, consistent and never invent anything:

MessageHandled as
A question matching one of your taught answersYour answer, sent word for word.
A standard question while the chat is idleThe built-in FAQ — 14 topics, English and Tanglish.
"M size 2", "1st one, L 3", "2 M 5", "XL" Read directly as product, size and quantity, and put in the cart.
"yes", "confirm", "seri", "ok" while confirming The order is placed immediately.

The confirmation shortcut only fires when the message is entirely a confirmation. "Yes but change the address" correctly goes to the AI instead.

8.2 The order flow

Customer asks for a jersey ↓ bot searches the live WooCommerce catalogue Bot shows matching products with price and stock ↓ customer gives size and quantity Cart updated [state: IDLE] ↓ bot asks for the shipping address Address collected [state: COLLECTING_ADDRESS] ↓ bot reads the full order back Customer confirms [state: CONFIRMING_ORDER] ↓ A REAL WooCommerce order is created ↓ Customer receives a direct payment link [state: IDLE]

The order appears in WooCommerce exactly like any other. If order creation fails, the bot falls back to sending a branded PDF proforma invoice so the sale is not lost. The cart holds one product at a time — a new product replaces the previous one.

8.3 Language

The language is decided from the customer's first message and locked for the whole conversation, so the bot never drifts between English and Tanglish mid-chat. Tanglish conversations are routed to an AI provider trained on code-mixed Indian text; English conversations go to a faster general provider.

8.4 Cold-lead follow-up

Every 30 minutes the bot looks for customers who went quiet. Anyone inactive for 3 hours or more gets a friendly re-engagement message, mentioning what was in their cart if anything was. Maximum two follow-ups per customer — it will not pester.

8.5 When you get pinged on WhatsApp

AlertWhy

8.6 When every AI provider is busy

The bot works down a chain of providers and keys automatically. If they are all exhausted at once, the customer gets a polite "we're getting a lot of messages" reply and their question is saved to a retry queue. It is answered automatically once quota frees up — and the queue survives a server restart, so nothing is silently dropped.

8.7 Missed messages — nobody is left unanswered

The bot only sees messages that arrive while it is actually connected. On its own, that would mean two groups of customers never got a reply: people who were already sitting unread on the phone when you paired it, and anyone who wrote while the server was restarting or down.

A catch-up sweep closes both gaps. Every time the bot connects, it checks every chat and finds the ones where the customer spoke last and never got an answer — that is exactly the definition of someone you owe a reply to. Those customers are then answered.

SituationDoes the bot reply?
A message arrives while the bot is connected and running Yes — within seconds.
Unread chats already on the phone when you paired the bot Yes — found by the sweep and answered.
Messages that arrived while the server was down or disconnected Yes — found the moment it comes back.
A chat where you or a staff member already replied from the phone No — correctly left alone. The bot only answers where nobody has.
A message received but not answerable (all AI providers busy) Yes — queued and retried automatically. See 8.6.

How the catch-up is paced

Answering a thousand-message backlog as fast as possible is the single most reliable way to get a WhatsApp number banned. So missed customers are split into two groups:

GroupWhen they wroteWhat happens
Still waitingWithin the last 12 hours Answered right away when the bot reconnects. These people are very likely still interested.
Older backlogMore than 12 hours ago Answered gradually — roughly two per minute, and only while no live customer is mid-conversation. A 1,000-chat backlog clears in about eight hours.
Three things that make this safe rather than embarrassing • Old messages are answered as old messages. Anything more than a day late opens by apologising for the delay, rather than replying as if it just arrived.
• Live customers always come first. The backlog pauses itself whenever someone is actually chatting, and resumes when the line is clear.
• Nobody is answered twice. If a queued customer messages again before their turn comes, the old queued reply is dropped and they are handled as a normal live conversation.
It survives restarts The queue of missed customers is written to the database, not just held in memory. If the server restarts halfway through clearing a backlog, it picks up exactly where it left off — which is the whole point of the feature.

You will be told. Within a minute of the bot connecting you get a WhatsApp message like: "Found 47 customers waiting for a reply — 6 recent, being answered now; 41 older, going out gradually over about 1 hour." A second message confirms when the backlog is fully cleared.

The one thing still worth doing yourself A very old backlog is answered, but slowly and by a bot. If there are high-value or angry customers in there, message them personally rather than waiting for their turn in the queue. The catch-up guarantees nobody is ignored — it does not replace judgement about who deserves a call.

8.8 What happens when many customers message at once

Every customer is processed in parallel, so thirty people messaging together are all understood at the same time — nobody is stuck behind anybody else, and nothing is dropped. The limit is on sending, not thinking.

All outgoing messages share one paced queue, because WhatsApp measures behaviour per account, not per chat. Replying to everyone instantly is the clearest possible automation signal and is what gets numbers banned. So replies leave at roughly one every 1.7 seconds.

Customers messaging at onceEveryone answered withinThe last person waits
30about 51 secondsabout 51 seconds
90about 2 minutes 49 secondsabout 2 minutes 49 seconds

Measured against the live send pacer with the current settings. Roughly 32–35 replies per minute either way.

Customers see "typing…" the whole time they are waiting The typing indicator starts when their message arrives and only stops when the reply actually goes out. A queued customer sees an active, busy shop rather than silence — which is why a 50-second wait during a rush is far less damaging than it sounds.

Where it starts to show: around 30 simultaneous customers the delay is barely noticeable. A reel drop putting 200 or more people on you at once is where the queue becomes visible. Photos cost double, because forwarding the image to you uses a slot as well as the reply, and ticket and escalation alerts share the same queue.

If large bursts become routine, the pacing can be raised — but that trades directly against ban risk, so it is a decision for your developer with real evidence from a real spike, not a default to change.

10 · Troubleshooting

SymptomMost likely causeWhat to do
Customers get no reply at all WhatsApp shows Disconnected, or safe mode is still on. Open the WhatsApp page. If red, wait for the QR and re-pair. If green but still silent, safe mode is on — ask your developer to clear the tester-number list.
Only some people get replies Safe mode, almost certainly. The numbers that work are the tester list. Have it cleared.
The old backlog is being answered slowly Not a fault. Older missed customers are deliberately dripped out to protect the number from a ban. Expected — roughly two per minute. Message any high-value customers personally instead of waiting for their turn. See section 8.7.
Replies are slow during a rush The send queue is pacing them to protect the number from a ban. Expected. Thirty simultaneous customers all get answered inside about a minute, and they see "typing…" while they wait. Only worth changing if 200+ bursts are routine.
No "missed messages" summary after a restart Either nothing was missed, or catch-up is switched off on the server. No summary means no missed customers, which is good news. If you expect one and it never comes, ask your developer to confirm catch-up is enabled.
A customer got a reply to a very old message Working as designed — the backlog sweep reached them. Nothing to fix. The reply opens by apologising for the delay.
"We're getting a lot of messages" replies Every AI key is in cooldown. Check the key list on Monitor. It recovers on its own; those questions are queued and answered automatically. If it happens daily, you need more capacity — tell your developer.
The bot ignores an answer I taught Keywords too weak, or wrong language setting. Add a two-word phrase keyword plus three or four single words. Confirm Active is ticked and the language is Both.
A taught answer fires on the wrong questions Keywords too generic. Remove broad words like jersey, order, price. Keep distinctive phrases only.
Uploading a PDF fails It is a scan, over 20 MB, or an unsupported type. Try selecting text in a PDF reader — if you can't, it's an image. Re-export from Word or retype into a TXT file.
Crawl says the site is blocking us A security plugin, Cloudflare rule, or maintenance mode. The on-screen message names the cause. It is fixed in WordPress, not here. Maintenance mode is a toggle in wp-admin.
Crawl finished but the bot still doesn't know it The crawl never reached the page with the information. Check the source card's page count. Delete it and crawl the specific policy URL at depth 0.
A source says "keyword only" Semantic search was unavailable when it was indexed. Once the warning at the top of the tab has cleared, delete and re-add the source.
The bot gives outdated information An old source or a stale taught answer. Search your saved answers first and fix or delete. Then check sources — turn one off to test whether it is the culprit.
Prices quoted are wrong A price was indexed from a document or old page. Delete that source. Live prices come from WooCommerce and should never be duplicated into knowledge.
Signed out unexpectedly The server restarted. Sign in again. Normal.
Console won't load at all The bot server is down, or the browser cached an old version. Try the backup address http://<server>:3000/admin. If that also fails, the server is down — contact your developer.
Red badge count never drops Drafts are being answered but new ones keep arriving. Healthy, if the questions are genuinely new. Dismiss the junk so the number stays meaningful.
Bot mixes up two similar jerseys Product naming in WooCommerce, not knowledge. Make the product titles and tags more distinct in WooCommerce and re-sync. No amount of teaching fixes catalogue search.
What to send your developer The exact customer message, the bot's exact reply, the time it happened, and any red lines from the server console around that time. Those four things solve most reports in one pass; "it's not working" does not.
